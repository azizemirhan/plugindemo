PHASE 6 / FU-1 - ROUND 2b ANALYSIS PLAN
=======================================

Fixed before the files were rendered. Do not read before rating.

These eight files are the round 2 targeted excitations with no tank:
same seeds, same centre frequencies, same ERB widths, same duration.
Four modes, two files each.

Per mode, the mean metallicity of its two control files is compared with
the mean metallicity of its two round 2 wet trials. With two ratings per
mode and one listener this is DESCRIPTIVE ONLY. No statistical or
inferential test will be run or reported, and no correlation will be
claimed.

Reading, fixed now:

  A) control 0-1, wet 3-4
     -> strong evidence that the tank adds the metallic character.

  B) control around 2, wet 3-4
     -> the excitation contributes; the tank likely enhances it.

  C) control 3-4, wet 3-4
     -> the targeted test's localisation is confounded: the excitation
        is already metallic on its own, and the targeted trials are not
        by themselves mode-specific proof.

The same descriptive comparison may be made for annoyance.

These bands will not be changed after the ratings are seen.

WHAT THIS CANNOT OVERTURN
-------------------------
If the controls also rate as metallic, that does NOT make round 2's
verdict wrong. Round 2's whole-tail context renders used an unfiltered
full-band snare - no band limiting anywhere - and still rated
metallicity 3 and 4, with annoyance 1 and 2 and one unpleasant. That
evidence stands on its own and is untouched by this control.

What a high control rating would weaken is narrower and must be stated
as exactly that: how strongly the targeted trials can point at any
particular high-band mode or sub-band. The existence of the character
and its localisation are separate claims, and only the second is at
stake here.
