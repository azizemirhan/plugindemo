  An empty overlap is not disagreement and is not counted as one:
  a trajectory whose matches all sit on one side of the anchor has
  nothing there for the opposite chain to contradict. It still has
  to be corroborated somewhere - two agreeing points across the
  three comparisons - before it can be called identity-consistent.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
  MEASUREMENT LABELS
-----------------------------------------------------------------

  Identity first, exponent second. A trajectory is identity-consistent
  only when it has four or more matched points, no reversal, no
  residual above three local spacings, no frame where an
  independently anchored chain chose a different peak, and at least
  two frames where one did choose the same. Only then is the
  modal coordinate read, and a CV below 5% called inverse-scale
  consistent. A low R-squared on its own is never called a tracker
  error, and a high one is never called a mode.
