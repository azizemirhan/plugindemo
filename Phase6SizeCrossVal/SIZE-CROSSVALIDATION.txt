PHASE 6 / FU-1 - SIZE TRACKER, INDEPENDENT VALIDATION
=====================================================

Source: c:\Users\Demirhan Özdemir\Desktop\Müzik\valhalla-reverb\Phase6ModalMap-v3\size-sweep-raw.csv
The tracker is not wired into ModalSweep, no classification is
changed, no full sweep is run, and the production DSP is read
through the same interfaces the plugin uses and never altered.

No gate, weight or ambiguity margin was moved for either half.

Under test: 49 identity-consistent trajectories.

Why every number below is paired with a decoy
---------------------------------------------
There is a detected peak every few Hz in this map, so a prediction
that lands near one has demonstrated nothing on its own. Each
prediction is therefore repeated at four frequencies known to be
wrong - offsets of -2.7, -1.3, +1.3 and +2.7 local spacings - and
measured identically against the same peak list. What is being
reported is the separation between the two distributions.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
  A. LEAVE ONE SIZE OUT
-----------------------------------------------------------------

  One interior Size point at a time is deleted from the tracking
  input and the whole sweep re-tracked from the same anchor without
  it. The prediction into the deleted point comes from the mean
  modal coordinate of the surviving matched points and nothing else.
  It is then resolved against that frame's full peak list, with no
  reference to what the complete tracking had assigned there; that
  assignment is read afterwards, only to score.

  Ends and the anchor are excluded: the ends would be extrapolation
  and the anchor is where the identities come from.

  bracketed (interpolation)  204 of 239

  A bracketed case is one where the reduced tracking had matched
  points on both sides of the deleted Size, which means its chain had
  to step straight across the hole - a scale ratio of 2^(2/3), 1.587,
  instead of the usual 1.260. If identity were being carried by
  proximity rather than by the model, the double step is where it
  would break.

  Read 'correct' for what it is. It means the prediction resolved to
  the same peak the complete tracking chose, which is a
  self-consistency check: both use the same model, so a model that
  was wrong in a consistent way would score here too. The decoy rows
  below are what make the result external - they share the model's
  neighbourhood and density and nothing else.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----------------------------------------------------------------

  Established: on Size points the tracker never saw - one deleted
  from its input, and four it was never run at - the inverse-scale
  prior predicts a frequency that lands far nearer a real peak than
  the same measurement made at deliberately wrong frequencies in
  the same neighbourhood. The prior is therefore carrying
  information about the tank and not about the density of the map.

  Not established: that a trajectory the tracker refused is wrong,
  or that a mode outside the gate's envelope does not exist. Both
  remain open and are reported as uncertain rather than resolved.
