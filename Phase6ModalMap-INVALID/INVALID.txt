INVALID FOR CLASSIFICATION - measurement-instrument defects
===========================================================

This sweep ran to completion and its raw data is kept unchanged, but three of
its measurements were reading the instrument rather than the tank. Nothing in
the columns below may be used as a production result.

DO NOT USE
----------

  modal_decay_s   The per-peak decay was measured by band-limiting to about
                  1 Hz and integrating. A filter that narrow rings for of
                  order 1/bandwidth seconds on its own, so the figure is
                  dominated by the filter rather than by the mode. It reads
                  4.0-4.1 x the broadband RT60 for nearly every one of 60
                  independent modes, which is not a physical result.

  decay_ratio     Derived from modal_decay_s. Same defect.

  class           All 60 modes came out class D because the Mod Depth A/B
  confidence      match failed for 57 of them. The reference list was
                  truncated to the strongest 60 *before* matching, so the
                  modulated counterparts were often not in the list to be
                  matched against. The classification is an artefact of that
                  ordering, not a measurement of the tank.

  size_fit_*      Only 5 of 60 modes were tracked over 4 or more Size points.
                  No interpolation was done, which was correct, but the
                  coverage is far too thin to classify from. The Size points
                  were chosen on the UI control, and the resulting scale steps
                  are uneven enough that the tracker loses modes between them.

STILL USABLE, with care
-----------------------

  The reference modal map itself: frequency, prominence, bandwidth, Q, and the
  resolution bookkeeping in RESOLUTION.txt. 3 s window, 0.667 Hz resolution,
  0.183 Hz display bin width, 2814 peaks above 10 dB prominence, broadband
  RT60 2.505 s.

  The raw per-setting CSVs, as a record of what was detected where.

  The render side is not in question: renders are bit-identical between runs,
  and the analysis/window-shift variability floor of 0.1278 Hz is a property
  of the analysis, measured separately.

STATUS
------

Superseded once the modal decay estimator has passed synthetic ground-truth
validation and the Mod Depth and Size defects are fixed. Kept for comparison,
not for use.
