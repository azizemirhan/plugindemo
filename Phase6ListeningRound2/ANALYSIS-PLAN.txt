PHASE 6 / FU-1 - ROUND 2 ANALYSIS PLAN
======================================

Fixed before the files were rendered. Do not read before rating.

Some trials share a mode; which ones is in the key. Per mode, over its
two trials:

  metallicity ratings differ by <= 1   -> repeat-consistent
  metallicity ratings differ by  > 1   -> perceptually unstable,
                                          reported as unresolved

A mode is a HIGH-PRIORITY PERCEPTUAL CANDIDATE when it is
repeat-consistent AND its mean metallicity is 2 or more, OR its
annoyance is 1 or more.

That label does NOT mean a DSP problem, and nothing in this round can
establish one. It means the character is perceptually salient and worth
attention.

A mode with high metallicity and annoyance 0 is reported as
"audible character, not objectionable". That distinction is the point
of asking two questions instead of one, and it is why preference is not
asked at all.

One of the four modes is structurally unresolved on the Size axis -
class D in the measurement. Whatever it does here, it will be reported
as a high-band perceptual candidate only. It will not be called
modulation-resistant or modulation-moving, because its structural class
is not known.

An outcome of "no metallic character anywhere" is a result and will be
reported as one. Nothing here needs a candidate to be found.
