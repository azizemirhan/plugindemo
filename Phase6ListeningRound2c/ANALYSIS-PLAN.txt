PHASE 6 / FU-1 - ROUND 2c ANALYSIS PLAN
=======================================

Fixed before the files were rendered. Do not read before rating.

Nine files: three full-band sources, each rendered DRY (no reverb),
WET100 (pure tank output) and MIX30 (the shipping Mix). No band
limiting anywhere.

This round decides one thing: whether the metallic character in the
shipping DSP is objectionable enough, in real use, to justify a voicing
intervention. It does NOT localise anything in frequency.

With one listener and one rating per cell, no statistical or
inferential test will be run and no correlation will be claimed.

THE GATE
--------
  A) WET100 metallicity high, but annoyance <= 1 and overall mostly
     neutral or pleasant
     -> audible tank character, NOT demonstrated as an objectionable
        defect.

  B) MIX30 shows, repeatably across two or more different sources,
     metallicity >= 3 AND (annoyance >= 2 OR unpleasant)
     -> shipping-context voicing issue evidence strengthened.

  C) MIX30 rates badly, but the DRY file of the same source rates
     similarly metallic or annoying
     -> source contribution or interaction is confounded; the tank is
        not blamed on its own.

  D) WET100 also reaches annoyance >= 2 or unpleasant across different
     sources
     -> tank-level voicing issue evidence strong.

These thresholds will not be changed after the ratings are seen.

THE VERDICT, one of three
-------------------------
  1. CHARACTER ACCEPTABLE - the metallic character is audible, and the
     evidence does not justify a voicing intervention. Stop trying to
     flatten FU-1; move to the Phase 6 closing assessment.

  2. VOICING ISSUE - a repeatable, objectionable metallic character in
     whole-tail and shipping context. Only then design the Size-based
     localisation experiment.

  3. UNRESOLVED - source, level and context effects still cannot be
     separated.

Any of the three is an acceptable outcome. Nothing here needs a problem
to be found, and CHARACTER ACCEPTABLE is not a failure of the round.

THE LEVEL CAVEAT
----------------
The three conditions sit at different loudnesses, because the
production gain law puts them there and nothing was levelled. The key
carries an analysis-only offset per file to the set median, applied to
nothing. Any reading that depends on an annoyance difference between
two conditions must be checked against those offsets before it is
believed.
